Our first game

Wordglow

Drag letter blocks onto the board, spell words across or down, and tap a glowing word to cash it in. Build TEA into TEASE for bigger points.

  • One simple rule: spell words with 3 or more letters
  • Levels that start easy and ramp up gently
  • Bomb blocks, 2x blocks and combo chains
  • A new Daily Board every day, the same for everyone

Sumburst

Drag number blocks onto the board and make touching blocks add up to the target. Hit it exactly and they blast. Go over and they stay stuck.

  • One simple rule: make the number, like 4 + 6 = 10
  • Targets start at 10 and grow slowly as you level up
  • Star blocks, bomb blocks, chains and quests
  • A new Daily Board every day, the same for everyone

Coming after that

Color Rivers

Draw a river from each critter den to its nest. When two streams cross, their colors mix, so red and blue make purple. Plan where the rivers meet to lead every critter home.

  • One simple idea: lead each critter home
  • Crossing streams mix colors
  • Bridges, underground channels, filters and deltas
  • A new Daily River every day, the same for everyone
