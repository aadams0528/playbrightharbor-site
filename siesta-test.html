How to play

  • Drag the sun along its arc. Every shadow swings at once.
  • Shadows point away from the sun. A low sun makes long shadows; a high sun makes short ones.
  • Tall things cast longer shadows: big trees are tallest, then trees, then rocks and parasols.
  • Win when every napper is in the shade and every flower is in the sun.
  • Each time the sun touches a napper it stirs. Think first, move once: fewer stirs, more stars.
  • Two suns: shade only counts where both shadows meet (the darkest patches).
  • Parasols: drag them from the basket onto the grass. Drag one back to the basket to take it away.
