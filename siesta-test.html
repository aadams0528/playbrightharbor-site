    How to play

    • Drag shade pieces from the basket onto empty grass, or tap a piece and then tap a square.
    • Drag the sun to preview any hour. Shadows point away from the sun: long when it's low, short at noon.
    • Each napper's nap hours are on the timeline. It needs shade for every one of them.
    • A flower's badge counts its hours of sun with your plan, out of what it needs (for example 7/8). It turns green when your plan gives it enough. Too much shade wilts it. Tap a flower to see which hours your shade covers it.
    • Tap a napper or a flower to see which hours the trees and rocks already shade it, before you add your pieces.
    • Tap Play the day. The sun crosses the sky and the garden is judged hour by hour.
    • Parasol: short shadow. Cypress: tall pot, long thin shadow. Shade sail: high up, its shadow lands away from it.
    • Restless pups: if the sun reaches a pup, it walks one square the way its arrow points and naps on, if that square is free and shady.
    • Stars: solve it on your first Play for ★★★, within 3 plays for ★★. A hint caps the garden at ★★, showing a square caps it at ★. Replay a garden any time to improve its stars (the daily garden counts your first solve only).
