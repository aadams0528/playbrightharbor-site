    How to play

    • Drag shade pieces from the basket onto empty grass, or tap a piece and then tap a square.
    • Drag the sun to preview any hour. Shadows point away from the sun: long when it's low, short at noon.
    • Each napper's nap hours are on the timeline. It needs shade for every one of them.
    • A flower's badge counts its hours of sun with your plan, out of what it needs (for example 7/8). It turns green when your plan gives it enough. Too much shade wilts it. Tap a flower to see which hours your shade covers it.
    • Tap a napper or a flower to see which hours the trees and rocks already shade it, before you add your pieces. A tapped napper also gets a row of hour buttons (🌙 shaded, ☀ still in the sun with your plan): tap one to jump the clock to that hour.
    • Tap Play the day. The sun crosses the sky and the garden is judged hour by hour.
    • Parasol: a short shadow. At 6 am it shades the 2 squares to its right, plus the 3rd square to the right one row lower. At 7 am, the square to its right, plus the square under that and the next one right of it. At 8 and 9 am, the square down and to the right. From 10 am to 2 pm, the square just below. At 3 and 4 pm, the square down and to the left. At 5 and 6 pm it mirrors 7 and 6 am, to the left.
    • Cypress: a tall pot with a long, thin shadow. At 6 am it shades the 2 squares to its right, then a long line along the row below. Through the morning the line swings down and to the right. From 11 am to 1 pm it shades the 2 squares just below. Through the afternoon it swings down and to the left, and at 6 pm it shades the 2 squares to its left, then a long line along the row below.
    • Shade sail: high up, so its shade is one square away from it, the square under its round dot. The dot swings from far right in the morning to just below at noon and far left in the evening. When the dot is off the garden, the sail shades nothing.
    • See it exactly: tap a placed piece and drag the sun. Gold dashes outline the squares it shades at the hour on the clock.
    • Restless pups: if the sun reaches a pup, it walks one square the way its arrow points and naps on, if that square is free and shady.
    • Stars: solve it on your first Play for ★★★, within 3 plays for ★★. A hint caps the garden at ★★, showing a square caps it at ★. Replay a garden any time to improve its stars (the daily garden counts your first solve only).
