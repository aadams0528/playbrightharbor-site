    How to play

    1. Fill the dotted picture with snow, exactly.
    2. Tap a cloud below to pick it. Press on the snow field to aim; a ghost shows where the snow will land. Let go to drop it. Slide off the field to cancel.
    3. A cloud's snow falls as one clump and stops when any part touches snow.
    4. Snow can't spill outside the picture, and it can't leave a hole under it inside the picture. Holes are fine where there are no dots, so you can build bridges.
    5. Undo is free. Hint always shows a move that works.
