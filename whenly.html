Whenly

A new history puzzle every day. Drag five moments into order, oldest at the top, then check. Three tries, a few clues, and a score you'll want to share.

Join the beta Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play
How to play

Five minutes a day

  1. Put them in order

    You get five moments from history, from the Magna Carta to the first iPhone. Drag them into order, oldest at the top.

  2. Check, then fix

    You have three tries. After each one, a green check means a pair is the right way round and a red cross means it's reversed. Stuck? A hint gives the century, then the decade.

  3. Score and share

    Get a score out of 100, read the story behind each card, and share a spoiler-free result with friends. Tomorrow brings a new theme.

Take a look

The same puzzle for everyone each day, easiest on Monday and hardest by Sunday. Missed a day? Play it in the Archive. Want more? Practice as much as you like, or try Expert and type the exact year. Streaks come with freezes, so one busy day doesn't break your run. No timer and no sign-up needed.
