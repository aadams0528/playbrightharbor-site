Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026. Updated October 11, 2026. Applies to Spellburst and other games from Bright Harbor Games.

The short version: you don't need an account, we don't ask for your name or email in our games, and your game progress stays on your device. Our ad and analytics partners collect some device information, as described below.

Who we are

Bright Harbor Games is a trade name of ABT Enterprises LLC ("we", "us"). You can reach us at support@playbrightharbor.com.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, level, coins, streak, word book and settings are saved on your phone. We do not receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Information collected by our partners

  • Advertising (Google AdMob). To show ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. On iPhone, we ask your permission before any tracking for personalized ads; if you decline, you see non-personalized ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK, we ask for consent before personalized ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase). We receive anonymous usage information, such as levels reached, session length and crash details, to fix bugs and improve the game. Test versions played on this website send the same kind of information through Google Analytics. It is not linked to your name.
  • Purchases (Apple and Google). In-app purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase happened.

Beta testers and Insiders

If you join Bright Harbor Insiders or a beta, you email us your first name, email address, phone type and the kinds of games you like, and you may send feedback. We use this only to send you test invites, ask about your experience and share Bright Harbor news. We never sell or share it. To leave, reply "leave" to any of our emails or write to support@playbrightharbor.com, and we will delete your details. Beta builds are installed through Apple TestFlight or Google Play, which handle your account under their own privacy policies.

How we use information

To run and improve our games, fix problems, show ads that keep the games free, and confirm purchases. We do not sell personal information for money.

Your choices

  • Limit ad tracking: on iPhone, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android, Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Remove ads entirely with the "Remove ads" purchase in the game.
  • Ask us a question or make a privacy request by emailing support@playbrightharbor.com. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have rights to access or delete information about you; we will help you exercise them with our partners.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
