Tide Turn

A tiny harbor puzzle. Three boards. This is an early test, so plain art.

  1. Every tide, each boat drifts one square along the current it's sitting on.
  2. Before each tide you may turn one ringed arrow a quarter turn clockwise. Or just wait.
  3. Get every boat to the dock of its own color.

The faint boat shows where each boat goes next. Undo and Restart are always there.
