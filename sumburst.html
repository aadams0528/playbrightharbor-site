Sumburst

Drag number blocks onto the board. When touching blocks in a row or column add up to the target, they blast. Easy to learn in one move, hard to put down.

Join the beta Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play
How to play

Three moves and you've got it

  1. Make the number

    Each level shows a target, like 10. Drop a block so touching blocks in a row or column add up to it.

    46

  2. Watch for gold

    While you drag, blocks that will blast glow gold. Go over the target and they stay stuck, so plan ahead.

    235

  3. Chain it

    Blast again within 2 drops to start a chain worth up to 3 times more. Longer sums score more too.

    19

Take a look

A quick three-lesson tutorial shows you the ropes, then levels start easy and ramp up gently, with bigger targets as you go. Star blocks that count as any number, bomb blocks, quests, a sum book that collects every way to make each target, and a new Daily Board every day keep it fresh. No timers, so you can think at your own pace.
