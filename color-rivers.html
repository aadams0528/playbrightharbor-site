Color Rivers

Draw a river from each critter den to its nest. Simple, until the streams have to cross. When they do, their colors mix, so plan where the rivers meet.

Join the beta Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play
How to play

Three ideas and you've got it

  1. Lead them home

    Drag a river from a critter den to the nest of the same color. The critters swim home along it.

  2. Cross to mix

    Where two streams cross, their colors mix. Red and blue make purple, yellow and blue make green. Some nests want the new color.

  3. Use less ink

    Find the shortest set of rivers for three stars. Every level has a best route waiting to be found.

Take a look

Short lessons show you each new idea as it arrives: bridges that let a stream pass without mixing, underground channels, filters, beaver dams and deltas that split one river into two. Hundreds of levels, a new Daily River every day, and no timers, lives or fail screens, so you can take your time.
