How to play

  • Tap empty water, not the animals, to drop a pebble.
  • The ripple reaches 2 cells up, down, left and right. Every leaf it reaches slides 1 step away.
  • Bring each critter home to the pad with its shape and colour.
  • Rocks and reeds stop leaves. Tied lilies stop ripples too.
  • Press and hold water to preview a ripple. Let go to cancel.
  • Undo and Restart are always free. Match par for 3 stars.
